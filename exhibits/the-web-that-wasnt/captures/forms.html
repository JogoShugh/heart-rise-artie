forms.html: everything you can do here

To do something, type its address into your browser. Put your own values where it says {like this}.

If it says POST, choose POST in your browser and type the body into the body box, exactly as shown.

1. LIST YOUR LOANS

Method GET
Address /cgi-bin/library.cgi/loans?card={card}&pin={pin}

What to type:

  • {card} -- your library card number, 8 digits, on the back of your card
  • {pin} -- your 4-digit PIN

Answers:

  • 200 -- your loans: loan number, book number, title, due date
  • 401 -- wrong card number or PIN

2. LOOK AT ONE LOAN

Method GET
Address /cgi-bin/library.cgi/loans/{loanId}?card={card}&pin={pin}

What to type:

  • {loanId} -- the loan number, from List your loans
  • {card} -- your library card number, 8 digits, on the back of your card
  • {pin} -- your 4-digit PIN

Answers:

  • 200 -- the loan
  • 404 -- no such loan

3. RENEW A LOAN

Only while it is renewed fewer than three times and no one's hold is waiting.

Method POST
Address /cgi-bin/library.cgi/loans/{loanId}/renew
Body card={card}&pin={pin}

What to type:

  • {loanId} -- the loan number, from List your loans
  • {card} -- your library card number
  • {pin} -- your PIN

Answers:

  • 200 -- renewed: due three weeks later
  • 401 -- wrong card number or PIN
  • 409 -- it cannot be renewed (the answer says why)

4. RETURN A LOAN

Method POST
Address /cgi-bin/library.cgi/loans/{loanId}/return
Body card={card}&pin={pin}

What to type:

  • {loanId} -- the loan number, from List your loans
  • {card} -- your library card number
  • {pin} -- your PIN

Answers:

  • 200 -- returned
  • 401 -- wrong card number or PIN
  • 409 -- it was returned already

Best viewed in a browser with a method menu and a body box.
