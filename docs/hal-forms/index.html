HAL-FORMS, HEART on-ramp

https://w3id.org/heart-rise/hal-forms

HAL-FORMS (application/prs.hal-forms+json) as HEART's on-ramp dialect, served at L0 only. Its templates are keyed by act name, and it is computed from the same legality decision as HAL Schema Forms, so both dialects always offer the same acts.
