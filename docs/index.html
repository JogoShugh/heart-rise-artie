HEART

https://w3id.org/heart-rise

The parent profile of HEART (Hypermedia Engine for Agentic Resource Transitions). A response names it beside the concrete dialect profile, in Link: rel="profile". It is never negotiated alone. A client that knows it knows that the response follows HEART's rules: state and legal acts in one representation, acts conditional on the representation's validator, and shared layers checked by integrity.
