RISE Minimal, End to End

The minimal RISE flow, every message in order

Envisioned, not built. Slice 1 of RISE, RESTful: a caller's reply, one job, progress, a signed outcome, then the refusals that follow. Each numbered step is one request and its response, headers and body. Act bodies are application/hal+json under the profile. The envelope is the request's own _links, with no _rise member. Keys, digests and times are made up.
