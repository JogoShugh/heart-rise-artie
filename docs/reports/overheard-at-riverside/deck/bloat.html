Overheard at RiversideA fictional conversation · plain page
Scene six · a month laterFiction

Isn't hypermedia bloated?

  • At L0 every answer carries its forms: about 1.3 KB per loan.
  • At L1 the forms move to one shared set per state, fetched once: 43% fewer bytes in all, loan answers 63% smaller, four more requests.
  • At L2 the schemas move out of the sets too; everything shared stays content-addressed and cacheable.
Priya

Fifteen people, every book, and the forms in every answer. Isn't that a lot of bytes? And a lot of round trips?

Sam

At L0, yes: every loan carries its forms, over a kilobyte each.

Sam

At L1, a loan carries a link to its forms instead. Every loan in the same state links the same set, at an address that never changes, so the client fetches it once and keeps it.

Priya

How many sets, for the whole company?

Sam

Four. At L2 the schemas move out of the sets too, shared the same way: two more requests, here.
