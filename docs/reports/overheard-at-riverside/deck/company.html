Scene six · a month laterFiction

A company of fifteen

A month later. The library has added company groups, and Harbor Point Logistics has signed up all fifteen of its staff.

  • A new release: company groups, searched by company, five members to a page.
  • A group member's book offers renew-for-member, which needs the card of the member it is lent to; any other card is refused.
  • One model run (56 calls) went through 3 pages, 15 members and 23 books, and renewed 14, each with its member's card.
  • The kept script loops over the members and their books, and reads the card from the member the loop is at.
Priya

Can the assistant renew for the whole company?

Sam

Once the model finds the way. It searched the members by company, five to a page, and went through all three pages: every member, every book.

Priya

A company's books are renewed for the member, though. The form asks for their card, and refuses anyone else's.

Sam

The model read each member's card number when it opened them, and sent it with each of their books.

Priya

And the script it kept?

Sam

Names no one, and no card. A loop over every member the search lists, page after page; inside it, a loop over each member's books; and the card read from whichever member the outer loop is at. If Harbor Point has twenty staff next month, it goes through four pages.

Priya

And Ana's script, from before the company groups?

Sam

Still runs. Her books are her own, so her forms didn't change.

What they wrote: company.goal.md 
Renew every book the members of a company group
have out that can be renewed, and say why for
any that can't.

- Find the members of the company group {company}
  - For each member, on every page of the results:
    open the member, and renew each book they have
    out if renewing is offered; otherwise say why
    it can't be renewed
What ARTIE kept: company.heart.yaml 
inputs:
  company:

steps:
  - act: library:search-members
    fields:
      company: ${company}
    then:
      - for-each: member     # every member, every page
        then:
          - for-each: loan   # every book each has out
            then:
              - when-offered:
                  - act: library:renew-for-member
                    fields:
                      card: ${member.id}

The goal says nothing of cards: the library's form asks for one, and the model took it from the member it had opened. The kept script reads it from the member the outer loop is at.

The run, and the library's release
  • The first run: Claude Sonnet 5.5, ARTIE at L2, for Harbor Point; 56 calls; 391,900 input tokens (247,234 sent, 144,666 read from cache); 14,453 output; 113 steps.
  • Kept by artie generate, which writes a list as one loop when the run opened every member of every page, following each page's next link; a member's own list is a loop inside it. The run sent each member's own /id as the card, so the script reads it from the member the loop is at. The same in Kotlin: each("member") { each("loan") { whenOffered … } }, the card read by val memberId by kept("member") { long("id") }.
  • Run with no model on a fresh library: 110 steps. The fifteen have 23 books out: 14 renewable, each renewed once on its member's card; 5 with a hold waiting and 4 at the limit, left. Three members had nothing to renew; two renewed two each. For the credit union: one renewed, one with a hold.
  • The release: search-members finds the members of a company group, five to a page, each page linking the next. A company group member's book offers renew-for-member in place of renew: its form asks for the card number of the member it is lent to, and refuses any other (422, at /card). The accounts search is as it was, so member.heart.yaml, pinned before the release, ran after it unchanged.
