Why this team cares

The Web made LLMs possible, and only half of it

Links everywhere

The Web’s architecture made every page reachable from another. A crawler needs no map.

A corpus

Filtered Common Crawl was the largest share of GPT-3’s training mix.

The missing half

Crawlers read. They never send a form, so the Web’s transactions were never learned from.

The transactional half of the Web is where agents now have to work.
