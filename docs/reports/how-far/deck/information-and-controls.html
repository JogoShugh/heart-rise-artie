Information and controls, presented together

“When I say hypertext, I mean the simultaneous presentation of information and controls such that the information becomes the affordance through which the user (or automaton) obtains choices and selects actions.”

Roy T. Fielding, comment, 20 October 2008

information + controls = affordance

On the human web they were married: the page and its forms arrive together. This is what lets an infer-once replay survive change.
