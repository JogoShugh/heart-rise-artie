Fielding, hypermedia and the generic client

HEART and ARTIE

The Web put controls next to information, so one browser fits every site. HEART does the same for programs and agents: each representation carries the acts legal on it now. ARTIE is the generic client that reads them, and turns one reasoned walk into a script that replays with no model.

Part 1

What HEART is

A media type and its rules, so a resource's representation carries the acts legal on it now. Absence is the enforcement.

Part 2

How ARTIE scripts and replays

ARTIE is to HEART what a browser is to HTML. A model may choose among what's offered; one reasoned walk becomes a script that replays with no model, held to what it was recorded against.
