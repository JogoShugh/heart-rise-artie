More Than Tokens: The Three R’s

RIGHT-BEHAVIOR

Only what the state allows. When an act is absent, a reason.

REPEATABILITY

That right behavior, the same way, every time.

RESILIENCE

The server changes, or misbehaves. The client takes the right failure path and carries on.

REASON is the hype. A reasoning model can stand in for a person in two places: finding the way the first time, and repairing when the controls change. In the middle, it’s the token maxing Hightower warned us about.

Repeatable: given the same inputs, the same outputs, call after call. All three together are what people mean by RELIABILITY; miss one and it’s gone.

Every part of this talk gets measured against these three.
