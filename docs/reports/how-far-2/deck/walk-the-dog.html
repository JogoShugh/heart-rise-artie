Walk the dog, ship the feature

Back to the dog. Built a few sentences at a time, one thumb free.

  • Claude Code’s /remote-control on the phone, a personal Max account at home.
  • Test first, every change: Gherkin, a red run, the code, a green run, a gated commit.
  • Evidence, not trust: the gates log every step, so what passed is on record when I get home.
  • The result: repeatable, reliable RISE behaviour, built on walks.
