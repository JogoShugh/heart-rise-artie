✓What a HEART client must do in HAL-FORMS, the L0 on-ramp21 scenarios · 36 cases · HalForms.feature

HAL-FORMS is one of HEART's two dialects (SPEC §6.1). It is served at L0 only, with each form inline under _templates and its fields in a properties array. A client that speaks it asks for it, and reads whichever dialect comes back. The garden serves here, and the build service, whose job "nightly" offers "build"; where a scenario says so, its template is the one written. A template's type is a hint for a person filling in a form, so a client refuses only what its other members disallow.
