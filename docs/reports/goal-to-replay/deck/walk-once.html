  1. Sync ARTIE fetches the system’s current resource: its state and the forms it offers now
  2. Reason Claude reads the line and the forms, and picks one, with its values
  3. Act ARTIE sends it; the system checks the values against its own form

Claude chooses only among forms on offer. It cannot invent a call.
