RISE Dynamic Webhook, Envisioned

The dynamic webhook, every message in order

Envisioned, not built. Slice 1 of RISE, RESTful: one loan, one reply, one watch, one answer, then the refusals. Each numbered step is one request and its response, headers and body. Defaults: act bodies are application/hal+json under the profile; the answer is held and linked. Names and digests are made up.
