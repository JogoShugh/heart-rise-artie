cancel

https://w3id.org/heart-rise/rels/cancel

On a RISE request's watch, while it awaits its answer: withdraw it, so no answer will come. A PATCH of the watch itself, application/merge-patch+json, with If-Match, whose standing is the const cancelled. 200 with the watch, cancelled. Once the service has answered, the watch offers no hr:cancel, its hr:unavailable says why, and a cancel sent anyway is 409 not-offered. A domain whose acts have side effects offers a compensating form here instead. Written as the CURIE hr:cancel.
