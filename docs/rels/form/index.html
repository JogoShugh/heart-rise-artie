form

https://w3id.org/heart-rise/rels/form

From an act's target to its form, where the form already lives: in the owning resource's shared affordance set, by a JSON Pointer fragment, with the set's integrity and the form's own. Served on the act's target when it is read, so the target has a representation of its own while the form stays shared. Written as the CURIE hr:form.
