Link relation

completed

https://w3id.org/heart-rise/rels/completed

On a reply, while it awaits: send the result; the reply is answered, at 100 percent, with no note unless one is sent. A PATCH of the reply itself, application/merge-patch+json, with If-Match, signed. Minimum schema: state, the const completed; result, in the shape the reply's owner names in its schema; _links with hr:in-reply-to. Replay: the same result sent again after the answer is answered 200 with the same ETag and records nothing; a different one is 409 not-offered. Other answers: 401, 412, 422 as for progress. The answered reply offers no forms and says why in hr:unavailable. Written as the CURIE hr:completed.
